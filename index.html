Angelina Gándara

"I went from flipping houses to helping neighbors — one family at a time."

I grew up watching my grandmother devotedly care for the homes she worked hard to save for; those same homes later became her only source of retirement income. About 6 years ago, I decided to take a leap of faith (with some careful planning) and follow her example by investing in fixing and flipping a house.

Today, I own and manage several properties in our city. The success I found inspired me to become a realtor, allowing me to help my neighbors become homeowners, too... and maybe inspire a few to venture into investment properties.
